# Postmaster brand assets

## Concept

**From digital thought to doorstep.** A folded envelope becomes a sheet in flight, suggesting the letter P through negative space. Forest green conveys clarity and steadiness; warm gold marks the moment a digital message becomes tangible. The portrait artwork follows a document from a glass screen through a flowing paper ribbon into a physical envelope and mailbox.

## Assets and use

- `app/assets/postmaster-mark.png`: original transparent PNG mark; site header, sign-in, sidebar, favicon and touch icon.
- `app/assets/postmaster-concept-portrait.png`: original portrait concept artwork; public homepage hero and customer dashboard banner.
- Wordmark: live text “postmaster.” next to the mark, preserving readable typography at every screen size.

Both original outputs were created with the built-in imagegen tool. No stock imagery is used. Logo distinctiveness is a design goal; trademark clearance is a separate process.

## Final prompts

### Logo — logo-brand

Create a distinctive premium logo SYMBOL ONLY for Postmaster, a digital-to-physical postal mailing platform. A single elegant folded envelope transforms upward into a flying sheet, with a subtle P shape formed in negative space. Compact, bold silhouette recognizable at favicon size, considered geometric curves, refined original identity. Existing product palette deep forest green #17644e with a small warm gold #d9b768 fold accent. Flat vector-like brand design, crisp edges, generous transparent padding, centered square composition. Transparent background. No words, no letters as typography, no mockup, no shadows, no gradients, no extra symbols. Deliver one polished mark.

### Concept portrait — stylized-concept

Premium website hero artwork for Postmaster digital-to-physical mail. Portrait composition 4:5. An enchanting tactile editorial paper sculpture: an elegant open forest-green envelope suspended above a small warm ivory architectural mailbox pedestal; a luminous ivory document flows from a minimal translucent glass digital screen above, through a graceful ribbon of folded paper into the physical envelope below. Subtle tiny warm-gold light particles suggest secure digital transfer. Sophisticated dimensional folded-paper art, fine paper grain, soft studio light and gentle contact shadows, restrained evergreen, sage, ivory and antique gold palette, warm ivory background #f3f4eb. Central composition with generous breathing space, visually clear at mobile size. Fascinating yet professional and trustworthy. No people, no words, no letters, no logos, no postal seals, no UI screenshots, no watermark. One finished artwork, not a collage or moodboard.
